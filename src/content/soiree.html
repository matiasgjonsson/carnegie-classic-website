Free for Competitors Carnegie Classic competitors get FREE admission to the Starlit Soiree!

Friday, October 9, 2026, 7–10pm.

Rangos Ballroom, Jared L. Cohon University Center

Kick off Carnegie Classic weekend with an evening of social dancing! Competitors, friends, and members of the local dance community are welcome to join us for the Starlit Soiree. Admission is free for all Carnegie Classic competitors.

Early competition check-in will also be available from 5:00–10:00 PM and is highly recommended for competitors. Competition volunteers will also receive free admission to the Starlit Soiree.
